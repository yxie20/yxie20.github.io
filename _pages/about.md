I am a PhD candidate at Caltech, advised by Professor [Steven Low](https://ee.caltech.edu/people/slow) in the 
[Department of Computing and Mathematical Sciences](https://www.cms.caltech.edu/). On the theory side, my work develops new tools for, and deeper understanding of, stochastic and integer optimization. Application areas include electricity markets, data centers, and distribution grid operations and planning. I have also led a large-scale hardware project deploying a high-resolution sensor network in Southern California's distribution grids.

I have worked in various industry positions, including quantitative trading in electricity markets and developing machine learning solutions for vision, robotics, education, and sustainability. I completed my undergraduate studies in Computer Engineering at Brown University, where I researched topics in robotics, reinforcement learning, 3D vision, and VLSI.
